## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an evocative, enigmatic cosmos built for intimate digital encounters under deep-space skies. It fuses **Futuristic Etherealism** with **Delicate Glassmorphism**, evoking wonder, quiet introspection, and tender magnetic pull. 

The aesthetic eschews harsh synthetic neon in favor of atmospheric depth: nebulous indigo mists, dusk-mauve star dust, and soft starlight glints set against obsidian voids. Interactive components feel like suspended relics or astronomical instruments drifting weightlessly in vacuum, responding with subtle luminosity rather than mechanical transitions.

## Colors

The palette is derived strictly from deep space voids, lunar dust, and muted cosmic mauves:

- **Base Void & Surfaces:** `#09070A` acts as the foundational abyss, supported by `#040D18` and `#0F0C14` for abyssal layers. `#192946`, `#1C2534`, and `#262632` establish elevated cosmic strata and surface containers.
- **Primary Celestial Glow:** `#6575A4` (Astral Periwinkle) drives primary actions, glowing orbital rings, and key focal points, supported by `#9AA8C4` for radiant high-emphasis states.
- **Secondary Romantic Dust:** `#A39091` (Stardust Mauve) introduces poetic warmth, soft badges, and intimate highlights, balanced by shadow undertones `#5C4A4C` and `#373038`.
- **Structural Steel & Midnight Accents:** `#253558`, `#30445E`, `#213448`, and `#2D3647` define structure, subtle glass borders, and containment cells.
- **Typography & Details:** `#9AA8C4` and `#858BA5` provide luminous, high-legibility textual clarity, while `#7E8595`, `#7E848C`, `#556482`, and `#5C5B67` govern secondary metadata and dimmed outlines.

## Typography

The type system blends avant-garde celestial geometry with tender, readable ergonomics:

- **Display & Headings (Syne):** Sculptural and eccentric, Syne provides an otherworldly silhouette that mirrors celestial arcs and planetary geometry.
- **Body & Longform (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Soft, rounded, and welcoming, keeping deeply introspective notes and conversational interactions gentle on the eyes.
- **Labels, Telemetry & Tags (Space Grotesk):** Precise, technical, and spaced out, evoking astronomical instruments, coordinate systems, and orbital telemetry.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial model relies on a floating 12-column fluid grid governed by generous negative space, mirroring the expansive stillness of interstellar space.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns, 1.5rem gutters, 3rem canvas margins. Content is grouped into floating astral clusters with generous breathing room.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 1rem gutters, 2rem canvas margins. Panels gracefully collapse into dual-column cosmic cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 0.75rem gutters, 1.25rem canvas margins. Structural navigation becomes an ethereal dock floating above the bottom edge.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through ethereal translucent layering and celestial radiance:

- **Surface Translucency:** Card and modal panels utilize `#192946` or `#1B2431` at 35% to 55% opacity with 16px to 24px backdrop blurs, creating deep celestial glass that reveals subtle drifting star fields beneath.
- **Ghost Borders:** Outlines are razor-thin (1px) rendered in `#394973` or `#556482` at 40% opacity, catching ambient light primarily along top and left edges.
- **Orbital Luminescence (Glow):** Replaces physical drop shadows. Elevated elements emanate a soft, multi-layered halo: an inner core tint using `#6575A4` (12% opacity, 12px blur) paired with an ambient cosmic bloom using `#253558` (25% opacity, 40px blur).

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, rounded contours (0.5rem base radius scaling to 1rem on cards and 1.5rem on dialogue viewports). Rounded geometries reflect the gentle curves of celestial bodies, orbs, and planetary horizons, softening the technical precision of space. Circular elements (aspect-ratio 1:1) are reserved for avatars, orbital progress nodes, and floating pulsar controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Celestial Action:** Backed by `#6575A4`, text in `#09070A`, with a faint `#9AA8C4` outer aura. On hover, the aura intensifies into a diffuse starlight halo.
- **Secondary Ghost Glass:** `#1C2534` fill at 50% opacity, enclosed by a 1px `#394973` border. Text set in `#9AA8C4`. Hover shifts the border to `#6575A4`.
- **Orbital Icon Buttons:** Circular containers (40px x 40px) with `#213448` semi-transparent backing, framing icons rendered in `#858BA5`.

### Chips & Constellation Tags
- Built with a delicate capsule shape (fully rounded pill).
- Background: `#1B2431` at 60% opacity with a 1px border in `#30445E`.
- Text styled in `label-sm` Space Grotesk, tinted in `#A39091` for emotional tags or `#858BA5` for navigational tags.

### Glass Cards & Celestial Modules
- Tiered containers backed by `#0F0C14` mixed with `#192946` via backdrop blur (20px).
- Delicately bordered in `#253558` with a subtle linear gradient fading into `#09070A` at the base.
- Internal padding strictly governed by `space-lg`.

### Input Fields & Star Coordinates
- Inputs feature dark crystalline wells styled in `#040D18` at 80% opacity, bordered by `#2D3647`.
- Text renders in `#9AA8C4` with placeholder text in `#40495D`.
- Focus states invoke a gentle `#6575A4` border transition accompanied by an ethereal glow.

### Celestial Orbs & Pulsar Toggles
- Custom toggles and radio selections adopt celestial phases: unselected states display a dormant lunar rim (`#3A3B49`), while active states illuminate into an iridescent orb (`#6575A4` into `#9AA8C4`) surrounded by a diffuse `#394973` corona.
